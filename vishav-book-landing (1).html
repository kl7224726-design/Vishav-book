Got questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Things to know before starting a handwriting project.

Can I do this work from home on paper/notebook? +

Yes. Projects are handwriting assignments that can be completed on paper or in notebooks from home. Confirm the materials and requirements with the team before registering.

How do I receive my project material? +

Project text and instructions are provided after registration. Ask the team whether your chosen project includes an online copy or a physical kit.

How and when do I get my payment? +

The reference platform states that payouts are made to a bank account or UPI after submission and review. Confirm the payment terms in writing before you begin.

Is there any age or qualification limit? +

The reference platform says no formal degree is required. Clear, legible handwriting is expected.

Can I take multiple projects concurrently? +

Contact the team to confirm eligibility and availability for more than one assignment.
